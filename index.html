COMMUNITY STRUCTURE CATALOGUE FOR MINECRAFT

Explore structures for Ancient Warfare 2

The Ancient Warfare Structures Encyclopedia is a community catalogue of downloadable structures and structure packs for the Ancient Warfare 2 (AW2) Minecraft mod. Discover builds, see who made them, and find packs to try in your world.

Maintained by TheOddlySeagull, this encyclopedia is a separate community website, not the mod itself. Its catalogue includes survival builds and world-generation structures, with metadata such as size, author, required mods, and generation settings where available.

Find a structure

Use the searchable catalogue to browse builds, illustrations, sizes and generation information. For a complete list of structure links without JavaScript, open the text-only structure index.

Check each structure's required mods and its source pack's instructions before installing. The catalogue does not guarantee compatibility with other Minecraft versions or AW2 ports.
